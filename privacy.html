Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Location Tracker may collect your username, account/session information, friend relationships, and device location information such as latitude, longitude, accuracy, and the time of a location update.

2. How We Use Location

Location information is used to show your location on the map and to let friends you choose see your current or last-known location when sharing is enabled.

3. Background Location

If you grant the required device permission, the mobile app may collect location while it is running in the background. The app requests updates approximately every 5 seconds, but your device or operating system may delay updates.

4. Who Can See Your Location?

Your location is intended to be available to friends you have added through the app. Only add people you trust. Do not use Location Tracker to secretly monitor another person or track someone without their knowledge and permission.

5. Storage

Location Tracker uses Supabase to store and process account, friend, session, and location information. A last-known location may remain available until it is replaced, removed, or otherwise deleted according to the service's data-management practices.

6. Your Choices

You can stop location sharing, remove friends, revoke device location permissions, stop using the service, or request deletion of your account and associated information.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to help protect information. No Internet service can guarantee complete security.

8. Children's Privacy

Location Tracker must not be used to secretly track children or anyone else. Parents or guardians should supervise a child's use of location-sharing features and permissions. Additional children's privacy requirements may apply depending on where and how the service is offered.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Significant changes may be communicated through the service.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, use the contact information provided on the Location Tracker website.
