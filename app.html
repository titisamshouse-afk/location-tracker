❓ Help & privacy

How does location sharing work?

Your location is sent to Trackit only while you use location sharing or send an SOS. You can stop sharing at any time.

Can friends see me when I stop sharing?

Trackit can show a last-known location when that setting is enabled, rather than a live location.

Does Trackit use Google Maps?

No. Trackit uses OpenStreetMap tiles and OpenStreetMap search features.

Where is local history stored?

Location history added by this feature stays in local browser storage on this device.
