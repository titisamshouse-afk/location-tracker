Location & Privacy

Location Tracker can let you share your current or last-known location with friends you choose. The mobile app can also use background location when you grant that device permission.

  • Your location is sent to our Supabase database when sharing is enabled.
  • Friends you add may be able to see your current or last-known location.
  • The mobile app requests location updates approximately every 5 seconds, but your device may delay them.
  • You can stop sharing and revoke location permission at any time.
  • Do not use the service to secretly track another person.
